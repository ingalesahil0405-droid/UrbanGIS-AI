## What are you building?

UrbanGIS AI is an AI-assisted GIS platform designed for urban planners, architects, researchers and GIS professionals. It combines geospatial analysis with natural-language AI to simplify land-use, accessibility, urban-growth and transportation analysis and turn GIS outputs into understandable planning insights and reports.

## What problem are you solving?

Urban planners often spend substantial time processing GIS layers, comparing spatial datasets and preparing technical reports. GIS software is powerful but requires specialized skills, while analytical outputs still need manual interpretation. UrbanGIS AI aims to reduce this friction by combining GIS workflows with AI-assisted interpretation and report generation.

## How will you use Claude?

Claude will provide the natural-language intelligence layer. It will help users interpret supplied GIS findings, explain spatial relationships, assist with analytical workflows, summarize project context and generate structured draft planning reports. The system will be designed so Claude does not invent measurements or map results that were not supplied.

## Current stage

Early-stage prototype. The initial prototype demonstrates an AI-assisted planning-analysis interface. GIS processing modules will be added progressively and tested against real planning datasets.

## Target users

Urban planners, planning consultants, architects, GIS professionals, researchers, universities, infrastructure consultants and municipal planning teams.

## Why Claude?

Claude's language capabilities can provide a natural-language interface over complex GIS workflows. The goal is to let planners ask questions naturally while keeping the underlying spatial analysis explicit and verifiable.

## Business model

The initial model is freemium for students and researchers, with paid plans for professional firms and organizations needing larger datasets, advanced workflows and automated reporting.
